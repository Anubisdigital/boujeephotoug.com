How Creator Rewards works

Budget. Create. Verify.
Reward.

The business controls the budget; creators compete through content performance; BoujeePhoto handles the campaign structure and verification rules.

01

Business launches a campaign

Choose the budget and campaign brief. The minimum campaign starts at 50,000 UGX.

02

Creators submit videos

Eligible creators create short-form videos for the campaign and submit them for review.

03

Verified views earn rewards

Creators earn 2,000 UGX per 1,000 verified views, subject to the campaign and per-video payout limits.

Budget control: 25% is the BoujeePhoto platform fee and 75% becomes the creator reward pool. The campaign pauses when the creator pool is exhausted or reaches its configured expiry.
