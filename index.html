How the math works
  • Boujee Platform Fee: 25% of your campaign budget
  • Runner Pool: 75% of your budget goes directly to paying Runners
  • Runner Rate: 2,000 UGX per 1,000 verified views
  • Max Payout Per Video: Capped at 10,000 UGX per video, regardless of campaign budget
  • Pool Expiry: Campaign automatically pauses when the 75% pool is fully paid out, or after 2 months by default
