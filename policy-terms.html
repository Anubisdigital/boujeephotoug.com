1. Who we are

BoujeePhoto is a done‑for‑you short‑form video production service based in Kampala, Uganda. We plan, shoot, edit, and post a month of short‑form video content for Ugandan businesses. Our service is a professional production and posting service — not a course, not a community, and not a membership.

We serve business clients who want consistent, high‑quality video content without the hassle of managing production themselves.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to enter into a binding agreement with BoujeePhoto on your own behalf. If you are under 18, you may only use our services with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who accepts these terms on your behalf.

By creating an account or making a payment, you confirm that you meet these eligibility requirements.

3. Payments

Clients reserve a filming date by paying a deposit of 200,000 UGX. The deposit is paid securely through Pesapal, which accepts MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Visa, and Mastercard.

If the client proceeds with the full monthly package within 7 days of paying the deposit, that deposit amount is fully credited toward the 900,000 UGX monthly package fee — no second payment is required for that amount.

The monthly package fee is 900,000 UGX. It covers content planning, one organised filming day, shooting, editing (captions, music, branding), and posting to one agreed social platform per month.

All payments are one‑time charges. Nothing is recurring. BoujeePhoto does not store your payment details; Pesapal processes the actual transaction under its own terms and conditions.

4. Cancellation & refunds

BoujeePhoto handles cancellation and refund requests on a case‑by‑case basis through direct negotiation between the client and BoujeePhoto.

There is no fixed refund window, no automatic entitlement to a refund, and no automatic entitlement against one. Each request is assessed individually, taking into account the work already performed and the circumstances of the cancellation.

To request a cancellation or refund, contact BoujeePhoto directly at the email address listed below.

5. No guarantee of results

BoujeePhoto guarantees the agreed production and posting service: professional content, delivered on time, in accordance with the scope agreed with the client. We do not guarantee view counts, engagement metrics, subscriber growth, sales, or any other business outcome.

Performance depends on factors outside our control, including platform algorithms, audience behavior, market conditions, and the client's own promotional efforts. We provide monthly performance reviews to help clients improve, but we cannot and do not promise specific results.

BoujeePhoto does not retain any automatic right to reuse, resell, repost, or publish delivered client content — including for portfolio purposes — without the client's separate, explicit, case‑by‑case permission each time.

BoujeePhoto never sells client data to third parties and never shares it for marketing purposes. Client data is used only to:
